## Corrections

- #4：v1 原话是 "adding interpersonal roles in prompts consistently improves the models' performance over a range of questions"，不是 "generally improved"；v3 的 "no better than random selection" 指自动挑选 persona 的策略（"with predictions often performing no better than random selection"）；v3 标题已改为 "When 'A Helpful Assistant' Is Not Really Helpful..."，引用须注明版本。
- #8（措辞精度，判定不变）：+80.8% / -70.0% 和 260 种配置出自 v3；17.2×、4.4×、180 种配置、+80.9%、"39-70%"、"~45%" 出自 v1 摘要与博客；v3 摘要把 45% 改成了 "certain performance"，45% 只留在 v3 引言。
- #12（归属，判定不变）："cognitive bolstering of the initial viewpoint" 是摘要对同一团队 "a prior study" 的转述，不是 Nemeth, Brown & Rogers 2001 本身的实验结果；本文自己的结果只有 "the authentic minority was superior to all three forms of 'devil's advocate'"。
- #14：应写作 "on one leaderboard dataset, models agree 60% of the time when both models err"，只适用于一个排行榜数据集。
- #15：应写作 "Agent teams use approximately 7x more tokens than standard sessions when teammates run in plan mode"；子代理模型解析顺序是 v2.1.251 起的顺序，之前 CLAUDE_CODE_SUBAGENT_MODEL 排第一，且 CLAUDE_CODE_SUBAGENT_MODEL_FORCE=1 可强制该变量优先。
- #18：Delphi 对传统小组应为 "five studies to one with two ties"（Rowe & Wright 1999 IJF 摘要与 2001 章正文 p.137 一致；只有 2001 摘要写 one tie）；"Heterogeneous experts are preferable..." 是推理出的原则，不是实证发现。

## Found but not cited

- https://iaorifors.com/paper/29874 ：Rowe & Wright 1999 (IJF) 摘要，是 Delphi 计票数字的一手来源；另称 "there is no consistent evidence that the technique outperforms other structured group procedures"，即 dialectic、NGT 等加了公开讨论的变体并没有稳定胜过匿名 Delphi，直接关系到"匿名理由反馈 vs 具名辩论"的设计。
